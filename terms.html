A few practical
things to know.

Updated 1 November 2026. FreePDFeditor is a free preview for preparing documents. No purchase or subscription is required.

Keep your originals

Output can differ from the original. Resizing and combining can change forms, annotations, accessibility structure, metadata, and signature validity. Image-based compression makes static images of pages; text may no longer be searchable or selectable. Always review important pages and keep an original copy.

No guaranteed target size or acceptance

Some PDFs cannot meet a requested limit with usable quality. The tool reports whether the measured bytes fit the selected limit. This is not a guarantee of legibility or acceptance by a website, employer, institution, or authority.

Supported use

Only process files you are authorised to use. Encrypted, corrupted, unusually complex, or very large documents may fail. Limits depend on the tool and your browser. This preview is supplied without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation or suitability for a particular purpose, subject to applicable law.

Open-source components

PDF creation and manipulation use pdf-lib (MIT). Rendering uses Mozilla PDF.js (Apache 2.0). Third-party licence notices are bundled with the application.
