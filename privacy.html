FreePDFeditor / PRIVACY

Your documents
stay with you.

Updated 1 November 2026. This describes the current preview version of FreePDFeditor.

Document processing

Selected files are read and processed in your browser. The application does not upload their contents to a server. Prepared outputs and queue information stay in browser memory and are cleared when you close or reload the page. Downloaded files remain wherever your browser saves them.

Network requests

The hosting provider receives normal website requests and associated technical information, such as IP address and browser details. The interface requests fonts from Google Fonts; that service receives the associated font requests. PDF libraries are bundled with the website.

No application analytics or advertising

This version contains no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, account system, payment system, or application cookies. The hosting platform may apply its own access controls. If advertising or analytics is introduced, these disclosures must be updated before those services are enabled.

Your device

Other software, browser extensions, shared-device users, and downloaded-file storage are outside the tool’s control. Keep original documents and check prepared copies before use.
